About Us

About Balin Optoelectronics

AIoT product design house — concept, hardware, firmware, AI and mass production, all under one roof.

Company Profile

Company profile

Balin Optoelectronics Co., Ltd. was founded in 2009 in Yongkang District, Tainan, Taiwan. We specialize in embedded systems, AI vision, LoRa / RF wireless communication and industrial automation.

We are more than an electronics design company — our customers see us as their AIoT product design center. We cover the full journey: concept, hardware design, firmware, AI algorithms, prototyping, certification testing and mass production.

With engineering skills that span several disciplines, we help customers turn new ideas into manufacturable smart products for industrial automation, smart monitoring, smart agriculture and IoT.

What We Do

What we do

One team covering every key step of smart product development:

  • Embedded Systems　Circuit design, firmware and drivers on STM32, ESP32, PIC and ARM Linux platforms.
  • AI Vision　YOLO object detection, OCR and edge AI deployment for production lines and field sites.
  • LoRa Wireless　SX12xx RF design and LoRaWAN networks — kilometer range on very little power.
  • RF Antenna Design　Custom PCB, IFA and patch antennas, tuned with a vector network analyzer.
  • Industrial Automation　PLC, RS485 / Modbus, CAN and MQTT — connecting machines and production lines.
  • Custom ODM　PCB layout, prototypes, pilot runs and mass production, plus testing and certification support.
