Applications

We combine embedded, wireless, AI and industrial control technology into solutions for real-world problems.

These are typical system architectures. Your solution will be designed around your site, budget and schedule.

Equipment condition monitoring

Challenge
Critical equipment such as boilers, motors and pumps relies on manual inspection rounds, so faults are often found only after a breakdown.
Solution
Add temperature, pressure, vibration and speed sensors, send the data over RS485 / Modbus or LoRa, and show it on a live dashboard with alarm thresholds.

Smart agriculture and environmental sensing

Challenge
Farms, greenhouses and outdoor sites cover large areas with no power or network, so cabling is expensive.
Solution
Battery-powered LoRa nodes measure temperature, humidity, soil moisture and water level and send the data several kilometers to a gateway and on to the cloud.

AI defect inspection on the line

Challenge
Manual visual inspection is tiring and inconsistent, and quality control cannot keep up as output grows.
Solution
We design the camera and lighting, train YOLO models to detect defects, run inference on an edge device in real time, signal the PLC to reject bad parts and keep images for traceability.
  • AI Vision
  • Industrial Automation

Label, serial-number and meter reading

Challenge
Part numbers, serial numbers and meter readings are still copied by hand — error-prone and hard to analyze in real time.
Solution
OpenCV preprocessing plus OCR models read text and values automatically and write them straight into your database or MES.
  • AI Vision
  • Embedded Systems

Wireless product and antenna integration

Challenge
Reception drops once the product is in its enclosure, or RF performance falls short just before certification.
Solution
We consider the antenna and mechanics from the start and tune the matching on the real device with a network analyzer, reducing redesigns and certification risk.

  • LoRa Wireless

IoT retrofits for legacy machines

Challenge
Existing machines have no network interface, and replacing them costs too much.
Solution
Add-on I/O modules capture machine signals and send them to the cloud over Modbus / MQTT, giving you uptime and fault data without modifying the machine.
