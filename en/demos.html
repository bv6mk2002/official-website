Architecture

Typical system architecture

  • Sensing: temperature, pressure, vibration, speed and DI / DO points via sensors and I/O modules.
  • Transport: RS485 / Modbus, CAN, LoRa or Wi-Fi, depending on distance and cabling.
  • Gateway: an embedded gateway handles protocol conversion, edge computing and buffering during outages.
  • Platform: MQTT to the cloud, database storage, live dashboards and alarm notifications.
