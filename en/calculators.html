Ask an engineer about these numbers

Method: sensitivity = −174 + 10·log(BW) + noise figure + demodulation SNR threshold; range uses a log-distance path-loss model; time on air follows the Semtech SX127x formula (8-symbol preamble, explicit header, CRC on, low data rate optimisation enabled automatically for SF11/SF12 at 125 kHz).
