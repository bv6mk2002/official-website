Core Capabilities

Six disciplines, one team

Hardware, firmware, AI and RF under one roof — fewer hand-offs, faster iterations and lower project risk.

Embedded Systems

Circuit design, PCB layout and firmware on STM32, ESP32, PIC, MSP430 and ARM Linux, including RTOS, low-power design and OTA updates.

AI Vision

Camera and lighting design, YOLO object detection, OpenCV and OCR, deployed on edge devices and integrated with PLCs.

LoRa Wireless

SX12xx RF design, point-to-point and LoRaWAN networks for AS923 / US915 / EU868 / CN470, gateways and MQTT cloud integration.

RF Antenna

Custom PCB, IFA, meander, patch and spring antennas, matching networks tuned with a vector network analyzer.

Industrial Automation

RS485 / Modbus RTU & TCP, CAN, PLC integration, multi-channel I/O and real-time dashboards over MQTT.

OEM / ODM

PCB layout, prototyping, pilot runs and mass production, plus test fixtures and certification (NCC / CE / FCC) support.
