FAQ

FAQ

Do you offer custom hardware design?

Yes. We offer one-stop custom development, from circuit design and PCB layout to firmware and mass production. You can also outsource just part of the work.

How long does a custom project take?

It depends on complexity. A typical controller board takes about 4–8 weeks from confirmed specifications to the first samples; projects involving AI model training or wireless certification take longer. You will get a clear schedule with our proposal.

Do you offer prototypes and pilot runs?

Yes. We offer flexible options from prototypes and pilot runs to mass production, so you can reduce early-stage risk and scale up as the market responds.

Can you do only firmware, or only antenna tuning?

Yes. Besides full development we take on individual tasks such as firmware development, debugging existing products, antenna matching and design reviews.

Which LoRa band should I use?

It depends on the target market: AS923 (920–925 MHz) in Taiwan, US915 in North America, EU868 in Europe and CN470 in mainland China, among others. Antennas and matching circuits are band-specific, so decide early — we can help you plan for multiple markets.

Who owns the design files and source code?

That is agreed in the contract. Design projects usually deliver schematics, PCB files, the BOM and firmware source code; licensing or manufacturing partnerships are also possible. We explain the options clearly in our quote.

How do I get technical documents and drivers?

Choose the document you need on the Support page and fill in the form. We send it personally to make sure you receive the right and latest version.

How quickly do you respond to support requests?

We answer general technical questions within one business day. Customers in mass production get a dedicated contact for urgent production-line issues.

Didn't find your answer?

Contact our engineers directly — we reply within one business day.
