AI Vision

YOLO object detection, OCR and edge AI deployment for production lines and field sites.

AI vision lets machines understand what they see. We cover the whole vision pipeline — camera and lighting design, image collection and labeling, model training and optimization, deployment on edge devices and integration with production-line I/O — so that AI moves from the lab onto the factory floor.

  • YOLO
  • OpenCV
  • OCR
  • Edge AI
  • Industrial cameras
  • AI agents

What we offer

  • Camera, lens and lighting design
  • Image collection, labeling and augmentation
  • YOLO object detection and classification models
  • OpenCV preprocessing and measurement algorithms
  • OCR for text, labels and displays
  • Model quantization and edge deployment
  • Results fed to PLCs, MQTT or databases
  • AI agent and large language model integration

Solutions

AI visual inspection system

  • YOLO
  • Edge AI
  • Defect detection

YOLO deep-learning models running on edge AI hardware for real-time object recognition and defect detection. Results go straight to the PLC for rejection or alarms, and images are stored for traceability.
