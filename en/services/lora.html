LoRa Wireless

SX12xx RF design and LoRaWAN networks — kilometer range on very little power.

LoRa transmits over several kilometers on very little power, which makes it ideal for wide-area sensing and remote monitoring. We design SX12xx RF circuits, antenna matching and protocol firmware, and support regional bands such as AS923 (Taiwan, 920–925 MHz), US915, EU868 and CN470 — from sensor nodes and gateways to the cloud platform.
