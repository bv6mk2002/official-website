RF Antenna Design

Custom PCB, IFA and patch antennas, tuned with a vector network analyzer.

The antenna makes or breaks a wireless product — and problems often surface late in development. We design PCB, IFA, patch and spring antennas around your enclosure, frequency band and use case, then match and tune them on the real device with a network analyzer, so your product performs reliably in its actual housing and environment.

What we offer

  • PCB antennas (IFA, meander, monopole)
  • Custom patch and spring antennas
  • Pi matching network design and tuning
  • S11 / S21 measurements with a network analyzer
  • Impact of housings, batteries and metal parts
  • Pre-certification RF review and recommendations
