Industrial Automation

PLC, RS485 / Modbus, CAN and MQTT — connecting machines and production lines.

Connecting equipment is the first step in a factory's digital transformation. We work with RS485, Modbus, CAN and Ethernet, connect to mainstream PLCs and sensors, and stream pressure, temperature, speed and vibration data to the cloud in real time — with dashboards that show managers what is happening on the line at any moment.
